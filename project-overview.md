# Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

MIND-COMMONS explores a community-governed approach to mental-health data stewardship in African communities.

## 2. Aim

To develop an open-source infrastructure that enables communities to participate in decisions concerning the collection, access, use and potential benefits of mental-health data.

## 3. Objectives

- Develop a consent-management prototype.
- Explore community-centred access governance.
- Design transparent audit and accountability mechanisms.
- Establish a participatory governance framework.
- Demonstrate the prototype using synthetic data.

## 4. Scope

The initial implementation will focus on governance and accountability mechanisms rather than clinical diagnosis, treatment or automated mental-health assessment.

## 5. Expected Beneficiaries

Young people, people with lived experience, community health workers, community organisations, researchers and public-interest institutions.

## 6. Development Status

Concept and initial documentation stage.
