# Community Mental Health Data Governance

## MIND-COMMONS: Community-Governed Mental Health Data Infrastructure

### Project Overview

MIND-COMMONS is a proposed open-source initiative exploring how African communities can participate meaningfully in governing mental-health data used in research, digital health systems and Artificial Intelligence applications.

The project seeks to develop a community-centred infrastructure that supports informed consent, data access governance, privacy protection, accountability and equitable benefit-sharing.

### Problem Statement

Mental-health data can be collected and used for research and AI development without adequate community participation, transparency or meaningful control over how the data is accessed and used.

MIND-COMMONS explores practical mechanisms for strengthening community authority and responsible data stewardship.

### Proposed Components

1. Consent management.
2. Community-governed data access control.
3. Audit and accountability dashboard.
4. Synthetic data for safe testing.
5. Community data governance framework.

### Intended Stakeholders

- Young people.
- People with lived experience of mental-health challenges.
- Community health workers.
- Community representatives.
- Researchers and academic institutions.
- Civil society and public-interest organisations.

### Development Approach

The project will adopt participatory design, privacy-by-design principles, responsible AI practices and open-source development.

The initial prototype will use synthetic data. No identifiable mental-health information will be collected or published through this repository.

### Current Status

Concept and initial project structuring.

### Expected Outputs

- Open-source prototype.
- Community data governance framework.
- Technical documentation.
- Consent and access governance resources.
- Research and learning materials.
