# Community Mental Health Data Governance Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework proposes principles for community participation and accountability in mental-health data governance.

## 2. Guiding Principles

- Community participation and representation.
- Informed and meaningful consent.
- Privacy and confidentiality.
- Transparency and accountability.
- Fair and equitable benefit-sharing.
- Responsible and trustworthy AI.
- Respect for human dignity.

## 3. Governance Roles

Proposed roles include:

- Community representatives.
- People with lived experience.
- Young people's representatives.
- Community health workers.
- Researchers and technical developers.
- Independent ethics and data protection advisers.

## 4. Decision-Making

The framework will explore transparent processes for approving data access, reviewing proposed uses, handling complaints and addressing consent withdrawal.

## 5. Accountability

Governance decisions should be documented, reviewable and communicated to participating communities.

## 6. Review

This framework is a working document and will be refined through participatory consultation.
